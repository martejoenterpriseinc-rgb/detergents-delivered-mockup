Refund policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026 · Mockup placeholder

We want every delivery to feel reliable. Here’s how refunds and replacements work in production (summarized for this mockup).

Damaged or incorrect items

Report within 7 days of delivery with a photo when possible. We’ll replace the item or issue a refund for the affected products.

Missing items

If something’s missing from your order, contact support and we’ll investigate. Eligible missing items are redelivered or refunded.

Change of mind

Unopened products in original packaging may be eligible for return within 14 days in production. Opened consumables are generally not returnable for preference alone.

Delivery fees

Delivery fees are refunded when we cancel an order or fail to deliver through no fault of the customer.

How to request a refund

  1. Open your order in Account (production) or message us via Contact.
  2. Include your order number and a short description of the issue.
  3. We’ll confirm next steps — typically within 1–2 business days.

This preview cannot process real refunds.
