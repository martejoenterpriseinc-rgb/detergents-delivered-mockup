Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about delivery, orders, and products.

What is Detergents Delivered?
We’re a household essentials delivery service focused on laundry and dish care — detergent, softener, scent beads, pods, and dish soap — brought to your door on your schedule.
Where do you deliver?
We deliver only in select areas of McHenry, Kane, and Cook counties in Illinois — not entire counties or all of Chicagoland. Check your ZIP to confirm coverage.
Is delivery free?
Yes — free delivery on orders $35 and up. Under $35, a $4.99 delivery fee applies in this mockup.
Do you offer same-day delivery?
No. We do not offer same-day delivery. Orders use scheduled delivery in select McHenry, Kane & Cook County areas.
What if an item is damaged or missing?
Contact us within 7 days and we’ll replace or refund eligible items. See our refund policy for details.
Is this the real store?
This site is a UI mockup preview for detergentsdelivered.com. Payments are not live and orders are not fulfilled from this preview.
How do I contact support?
Reach us via the contact form. In production, you’d also get email and in-app chat support.
